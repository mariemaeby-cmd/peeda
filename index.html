No ESI facility found in your area

Under the ESI Corporation's policy (adopted at its 183rd Board Meeting, 07.12.2020), if no ESI hospital, dispensary, or Insurance Medical Practitioner is available within 10 km of your residence, you're entitled to visit any ESIC-empanelled hospital directly — no referral required. Bring your ESI e-Pehchan Card / Health Passbook along with your Aadhaar or another government ID.

This is grounded in Section 56(1) of the ESI Act, 1948, which obliges the provision of medical benefit to insured persons.
